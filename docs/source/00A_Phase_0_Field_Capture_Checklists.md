## Operator interview prompts

Ask the operator:

1. What usually causes the wrong size, colour or garment to be used?
2. Which information is often missing when production begins?
3. How is customer approval currently proved?
4. How is artwork mirrored and checked?
5. Which cutter settings change by material?
6. What happens when a cut fails?
7. How is material waste estimated?
8. How is transfer placement measured?
9. Which press settings are most likely to cause damage?
10. How is rework recorded and charged?
11. Which steps require a manager's approval?
12. Which information should appear on the operator's screen and which should remain hidden?
13. What should happen when internet access is unavailable?
14. What evidence is needed before marking a job complete?
15. Which reports would help reduce waste, delays and rework?

## Evidence handling rules

- Do not store customer secrets in public documentation.
- Mask serial numbers in screenshots shared publicly when necessary.
- Keep original machine-label and Device Manager photographs in restricted project evidence storage.
- Record the date and person who captured every technical value.
- Replace an old value only after a controlled retest.
- Keep failed test results; they are part of the safety record.
